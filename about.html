Our story

Curated South-India travel, done properly.

Sanchari began with a simple question — why is it easier for an Indian traveller to plan a European trip than a Chola-temple circuit? We built a company to fix that.

Today, we run pilgrimage circuits, adventure escapes, and Ayurvedic retreats across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh — with our own ground crew in every region.
